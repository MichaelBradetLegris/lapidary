title: Chapter 1 - The Flood
status: draft
---

It was the third night of rain, and the river had began to climb the steps below the mill. Mara counted them the way she counted everything now, in twos, and when she reached the fourth step she stopped counting altogether.

"You should of told me," she said.

Her brother did not look up from the rope. He had been splicing the same length for an hour, and the the frayed end had gone soft and grey in his hands. "Told you what," he said. It was not a question.

"That the water was coming. That we would loose the lower field."

"You knew."

She nodded her head. She had known. Knowing was not the same as being told, and she wanted very badly to be angry with someone who was standing close enough to hear it.

The lamp guttered. Outside, the sound of the river had changed sometime in the last hour, from a rush to something lower and more patient, and neither of them had noticed when. Its a sound she would remember for thirty years, and she would never once be able to describe it to anyone who had not stood in that room.

"Tomorrow we move the ewes," he said finally. "Then we see what's left."

Mara looked at the door. The gap beneath it was dark and wet, and while she watched, a thin tongue of water come across the flagstones toward her boot, paused, and went no further.

She did not move her foot. It seemed important, then, to not move her foot.
